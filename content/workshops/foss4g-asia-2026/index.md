As a Training Associate at [Spatial Thoughts](https://spatialthoughts.com), I create and deliver hands-on training in Google Earth Engine (basic to advanced), Spatial Analysis with Python, QGIS, and command-line geospatial tools like GDAL.

At FOSS4G Asia, this included leading a session built around spatial-SQL notebooks — using **DuckDB** to query GeoParquet and GeoNames data live, then visualising the results with Folium and Matplotlib (see the [DuckDB Geospatial Analysis](/projects/duckdb-geospatial-analysis/) and [Interactive GeoNames Map](/projects/interactive-geonames-map/) projects for the underlying notebooks).
