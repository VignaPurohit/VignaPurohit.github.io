## Context

Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) studies for the Indian Oil Corporation Limited (IOCL) refinery at Panipat, conducted as part of a project team at NEERI (CSIR), where EIA studies are one of the Institute's core consultancy services for industrial and infrastructure projects.

## Approach

Contributed to the environmental baseline and impact assessment work supporting the refinery's EIA process — part of the broader project-coordination and field-research experience gained at NEERI alongside GIS-based risk modelling and land-cover mapping work.
