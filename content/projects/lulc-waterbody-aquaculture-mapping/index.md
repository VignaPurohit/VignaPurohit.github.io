## Context

A set of remote-sensing-based mapping projects carried out at NEERI, supporting environmental monitoring across three related problems: classifying land use and land cover change, identifying and mapping waterbodies, and extracting aquaculture ponds from satellite imagery.

## Approach

Satellite imagery classification and spatial analysis to delineate land cover classes, surface waterbodies, and aquaculture ponds — feeding into the Institute's broader environmental monitoring and assessment work.
