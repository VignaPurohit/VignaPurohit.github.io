## The Question

Forest fire damage in India is significant, and most incidents go unreported until after the fact. Could a near real-time, satellite-based system flag fires as they start — fast enough for forest authorities to actually act on?

## Context

My M.Sc Geoinformatics dissertation, focused on the **Gadchiroli district of Maharashtra** — a largely forested, under-monitored region where both natural and manmade fires occur frequently.

## Data

MODIS satellite data at 1 km spatial resolution, together with a historical record of fire incidents in the district used to characterise typical fire behaviour in the area.

## Approach

Developed an algorithm, grounded in historical fire incidents and fire behaviour patterns, to detect hotspots from incoming MODIS data and distinguish likely fire events from noise.

## Outcome / Impact

Detected hotspots were relayed to forest authorities by email, providing a near real-time alert that included the location information of each fire incident — turning a satellite data feed into an actionable early-warning channel rather than a static map.

## Technical Details

MODIS fire/thermal anomaly products, Python-based geospatial processing, automated email alerting.
