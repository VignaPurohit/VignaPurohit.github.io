## My Approach

I start with the question, not the toolkit. A good spatial analysis moves from understanding what's actually being asked, through exploring and cleaning the data, to designing a workflow that someone else could rerun — then communicating the result clearly enough that it actually gets used. Reproducibility and clarity matter to me as much as the analysis itself.

## My Journey

I came to geospatial work sideways — a mathematics degree first, then an M.Sc in Geoinformatics, where a dissertation on early warning forest-fire detection and a research internship on Antarctic sea-ice variability pulled me firmly into remote sensing. From there: environmental research at NEERI (a CSIR laboratory), building production GIS tooling at Genesys International, and now teaching and consulting as a Training Associate at Spatial Thoughts.

## Currently

Based in Tallinn, Estonia, with roots in Gujarat, India. I design and deliver geospatial training — Google Earth Engine, Python, QGIS — and work on consultancy projects spanning risk modelling, environmental monitoring, and spatial data engineering.

## What I Like to Build

Decision-support tools people can actually use — like a QGIS-based risk assessment application for water infrastructure. Dependency-light spatial-SQL workflows with DuckDB. Interactive maps that make a dataset explorable rather than just downloadable. Training material that turns a complex workflow into something a room full of people can follow.
